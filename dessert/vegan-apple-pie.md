# Vegan Apple Pie

## Ingredients

### Crust:
* 2 cups flour
* 1 tsp salt
* ⅔ cup shortening
* 6-7 tbsp cold water

### Filling:
* 4 cups granny smith apples
* 1 cup cane sugar
* ½ tsp salt
* ½ tsp cinnamon
* zest of half lemon
* 1 tbsp lemon juice
* 1 tbsp vegan butter

### Topping:
* Non-dairy milk
* A pinch of cane sugar

## Directions
1) Preheat oven to 450°F.
2) In a large bowl, combine flour salt and shortening. Cut shortening into flour with a fork or pastry cutter.
3) Slowly, adding one tbsp at a time, mix water into the bowl and form dough into a ball.
4) Divide the ball in two halves. Wrap one half and place in the refrigerator to cool. Flour surface and roll the other half into a circle until it is about ¼ inch thick.
5) Place the rolled out crust into your pie pan and refrigerate.
6) Peel the apples, be careful. 
7) Cut the apples into thin slices, about ¼ inch thick.
8) In a new bowl, combine the apples, sugar, salt, cinnamon, zest and lemon juice. Mix well. 
9) Take out your pie crust. Neatly arrange the apples in the pan starting from the center, working in a circle, until you get close to the edges of the crust. Repeat with the rest of the apples to create layers. Filling should be tallest in the center and taper out towards the sides. 
10) Dot the pie filling with butter.
11) Roll the other half of the crust. Check to make sure it will cover the entire pie.
12) Place over the filling and crimp around the edges to seal it.
13) Cut 5 slits/vents radiating out through from the center 2 inches long each. Cut 5 more slits 1 inch long in between the others. It should look like a starburt or sparkle when done. 
14) Brush non-dairy milk onto the pie crust in place of an eggwash. Sprinkle a bit of cane sugar on top. 
15) Bake at 450°F for 10 minutes. 
16) Reduce heat to 350°F and bake for 30 minutes. 
17) Let cool, and enjoy!
