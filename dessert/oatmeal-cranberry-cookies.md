# Oatmeal Cranberry Cookies

## Ingredients

* 1 tbsp ground flaxseed
* 3 tbsp water
* 1 cup all-purpose flour
* ½ tsp baking soda
* ¼ tsp cinnamon
* ¼ tsp coarse salt
* ½ cup vegan butter, softened
* ⅓ cup + 2 tbsp brown sugar
* ⅓ cup + 2 tbsp sugar
* 1 tsp vanilla extract
* 1½ cup old-fashioned oats
* ¾ cup dried cranberries
* ¼ cup pecans, crushed

## Directions

1) Preheat oven to 350˚F, line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2) In a small bowl, mix flaxseed and water together. Set aside.
3) In a medium bowl, whisk the flour, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt together.
4) With an electric mixer, beat the butter and sugars at a medium speed until light and fluffy.
5) Add vanilla, and the flax mixture to the bowl, and mix until incorporated.
6) Slowly add the flour mixture to the bowl, mixing until each addition has been incorporated.
7) Mix in oats and raisins until evenly distributed.
8) Place 2 tablespoon balls of dough onto the prepared baking sheet. 
9) Bake for 10 – 11 minutes, or until the dough has just set on the top.
10) Remove from oven and allow the cookies to cool on the baking sheet for a few minutes before transferring to a cooling rack.
