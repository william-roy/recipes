# Panang Curry

## Ingredients
* 1 small onion, chopped
* 1 green bell pepper, sliced
* 1 red bell pepper, sliced
* 2 tsp freshly grated ginger
* 4 garlic cloves, minced
* 1 tbsp oil
* 2 tbsp panang curry paste
* 1 tbsp peanut butter
* 1 can coconut milk
* 2 tsp cornstarch 
* ¼ cup brown sugar
* 1 tbsp fish sauce
* 1 tbsp lime juice
* 1 cup thai basil leaves
* Salt and pepper, to taste

## Directions
1) Cut tofu into wedge shapes (approx. 2" x 0.5"). Fry in about 0.75" of canola oil, transferring to a plate when crispy and golden brown.
2) In a larger pan or wok, add oil and heat medium-high. Add onions and sautee for 2 minutes.
3) Add bell peppers, garlic, and ginger. Sautee for another 2 minutes.
4) Add curry paste and peanut butter and sautee for 1 minute. 
5) Add 1/2 can of coconut milk and stir. Add cornstarch to can and stir before adding to the pan.
6) Stir in sugar, fish sauce, lime juice, and 3/4 of the basil leaves. Reduce heat and simmer for 5 minutes.
7) Add salt and pepper to taste, serve over white rice.
