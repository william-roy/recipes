# Szechuan Tofu Stir fry

## Ingredients
* 1 block extra firm tofu
* 3 tbsp soy nuts
* 1½ tbsp Lao Gan Ma spicy chili crisp
* 1½ inches ginger, diced
* 6-8 scallions, chopped
* ¼ tsp msg
* 1 tbsp olive oil
* 1 tbsp soy sauce
* 1 tbsp rice vinegar
* 1 tsp sesame oil
* ¼ tsp ground Szechuan Pepper

## Directions
1) Pre-heat oven to 425°F, press tofu to remove excess moisture.
2) Cut tofu roughly into ½ inch x ½ inch pieces and spread out on a parchment lined pan. Bake for 10 minutes.
3) Remove tofu from oven and add oil to large pan on medium high heat.
4) Add tofu to the pan, followed by soy nuts, Szechuan pepper, rice vinegar, and soy sauce. Mix and cook for 5-10 minutes (will depend on how firm or moist tofu is).
5) Add spicy chili crisp, sesame oil, msg, scallions, and ginger. Mix and cook for another 5-10 minutes.
