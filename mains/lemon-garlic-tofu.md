# Lemon Garlic Tofu

## Ingredients
* 1 block of extra firm tofu
* 1 lemon
* 4 cloves of garlic
* black pepper
* ¼ tsp salt
* ½ tbsp oregano
* 1 tbsp rosemary
* 1 tbsp thyme
* ¼ tsp msg
* 1 tbsp olive oil

## Directions
1) Pre-heat oven to 425°F, press tofu to remove excess moisture
2) Cut the tofu into small strips, approximately 1" x 3" and spread out on a parchment lined pan. Bake for 10 minutes.
3) Add oil and garlic to large pan and sautee on medium heat until fragrant.
4) Reduce heat slightly and add the tofu to the pan. Cook for 5 minutes.
5) Mix in black pepper, salt, msg, oregano, rosemary, thyme, and lemon.
6) Cook for another 5-10 minutes.
