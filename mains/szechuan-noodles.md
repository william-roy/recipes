# Szechuan Noodles

## Ingredients
* 6 tbsp roasted unsalted peanutes
* 1 tbsp Doubanjiang
* 1 block firm tofu
* 5 or 6 shiitake mushrooms
* 4 scallions, sliced
* ½ tbsp chinese sesame paste
* 1 tsp soy sauce
* 2 tbsp chili oil
* 1 tsp spicy chili crisp
* 1 tsp sugar
* 3 garlic cloves
* 2 tbsp ginger
* ¾ cup water
* 7oz noodles

## Directions
1) Heat some oil in a large frying pan and stir-fry the chilli bean paste until the oil is bright red and the paste smells aromatic. Transfer it to a mixing bowl.
2) Squeeze the tofu between sheets of kitchen paper to remove as much water as possible. Then use your fingers to crumble it into a small mince. 
3) Heat some more oil in the same frying pan you used before, then fry the minced tofu on medium-high heat until golden and set aside. Finely chop the mushrooms and fry them in oil until golden and set aside as well. Bring a pan of seasoned water to a boil for the noodles.
4) In the meantime, you can finish the paste. To the mixing bowl with the chilli bean paste, add the sesame paste or tahini, soy sauce, chilli oil, chilli sediment and sugar. Grate in the garlic and ginger and mix it into a paste, then add the water and stir to combine. Heat up the frying pan once more and pour in the sauce. Let it come to a boil, then turn off the heat and stir in most of the spring onions followed by the tofu, mushrooms and peanuts.
5) Cook the noodles according to the packaging instructions, then add to the pan and stir to mix well. Divide everything over two bowls or deep plates and garnish with the remaining spring onions.
