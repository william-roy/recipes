# Tofu Shawarma Wraps

## Ingredients
* 2 pitas, warmed
* ½ red onion
* mint leaves
* 3 tbsp rice vinegar
* 2 small cucumbers

### Tofu
* 1 block extra firm tofu
* 3 tbsp olive oil
* 2 tsp coriander
* 2 tsp paprika
* ½ tsp crushed red pepper
* 1 tsp kosher salt
* ⅛ tsp cinnamon

### Sauce
* ½ cup non-dairy yogurt
* 2 cloves garlic, finely grated
* 1 tbsp lemon juice (~½ lemon)
* salt, to taste

## Directions
1) Pre-heat oven to 425°F. Slice onion into fine strips and combine with rice vinegar in a small bowl.
2) In a large bowl, tear up tofu into bite sized pieces and add olive oil, coriander, paprika, crushed red pepper, kosher salt, and cinnamon. Mix thoroughly.
3) Spread contents of bowl onto a lined baking sheet and bake for 20-25 minutes.
4) In a small bowl, combine yogurt, garlic, lemon juice, and salt.
5) Slice cucumbers lengthwise into strips.
6) When tofu is nearly done baking (~3 minutes before), wrap pitas in foil and add to oven to warm.
7) Remove tofu and pita from oven. On each pita add sauce, tofu, cucumber, onions, and mint. Wrap in foil and serve warm.
