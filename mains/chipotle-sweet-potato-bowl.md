# Chipotle Sweet Potato Bowl

## Ingredients
* 2 sweet potatoes
* 1 poblano pepper
* 1 bell pepper
* cilantro
* 1 can organic black beans
* 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
* 1½ tsp smoked paprika
* 1½ tsp ground cumin
* 1 tsp chipotle chili powder
* 1 tsp kosher salt
* ¼ tsp crushed red pepper

### Lime Crema
* ½ cup vegan sour cream
* 3 tbsp vegan mayonnaise
* 1 lime
* sea salt

## Directions
1) Pre-heat oven to 425°F and chop sweet potatoes and peppers into bit sized pieces and add to a large mixing bowl.
2) Add olive oil, paprika, cumin, chili powder, kosher salt, and red pepper to the bowl and mix thoroughly.
3) Spread contents of bowl onto lined baking sheet and put in oven for 20-30 minutes (this seems to vary a lot based on how big you chopped the ingredients and how hot your oven runs).
4) In a small bowl, mix vegan sour cream and mayonnaise. Add the zest of one lime and sea salt to taste.
5) Drain and rinse black beans and heat them in a small pan with water and a dash of salt.
6) When sweet potatoes are tender and slightly scorched, remove from oven and combine with cilantro and black beans in a large bowl. Mix thoroughly and serve hot.
