# Vegan Ramen

## Ingredients
* 4 cups vegetable broth
* ¼ cup tahini
* 2 tbsp white miso paste
* 1 tbsp maple syrup
* 3 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 yellow onion, diced
* 1 tbsp sesame oil
* 2 bricks of ramen noodles

### Toppings
* silken tofu
* scallions
* shiitake mushrooms
* crispy fried onions
* sesame seeds
* chili oil
* vegetable of your choice (spinach, yu choy, bok choy, etc.)

## Directions
1) Sautee onion and garlic in a large pot until tender and fragrant
2) Add 1 cup of vegetable broth, tahini, miso paste, maple syrup. Stir until well incorporated.
3) Add remaining vegetable broth. Stir well and cover. Simmer for 20 mins, stirring occasionally. 
4) In a separate pot, cook ramen noodles according to package instructions (usually 2 cups water per brick, for ~4 minutes)
5) Strain the broth. Add noodles to serving bowls and add broth and desired toppings.
