# Miso Curry Squash

## Ingredients
* 1 block extra firm tofu
* 1 delicata squash (or acorn squash)
* 1 potato
* ⅔ cup fresh cilantro
* 1 ½ cup chopped kale
* ⅓ cup pepitas

## Sauce
* ¼ cup olive oil
* ¼ cup white miso paste
* 1 tbsp red curry paste
* 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice

## Directions
1) Pre-heat the oven to 400°F
2) Cut delicata squash in half lengthwise and remove seeds, then cut into 1/2 inch moons and add to large mixing bowl. Peel and cut potato into small (~1/2 inch) chunks and add to the bowl. Do the same with the tofu.
3) In a separate small mixing bowl create the sauce by whisking together oil, miso, and curry paste.
4) Add about 2/3 of the sauce to large mixing bowl and mix thoroughly.
5) Spread the ingredients across a parchment-lined baking pan and put in the oven to roast for about 25-30 minutes. About halfway through, turn the incredient to ensure they're cooked evenly. About 5 minutes before removing them, you can add pepitas to the pan to toast them.
6) Whisk lemon juice into the remaining sauce mixture.
7) Add washed/massaged/chopped kale to large mixing bowl.
8) Remove pan from the oven and add contents to the mixing bowl, along with remaining sauce and cilantro. Mix thoroughly and serve warm.
