# Vegan Pizza

## Ingredients

### Red sauce:
* 1 can (15 oz) crushed tomatoes
* 1 small can (5-6 oz) tomato paste
* fresh basil
* 1 tbsp oregano
* ½ tbsp salt (or garlic salt)
* 1 tsp sugar
* ½ tsp garlic powder
* ½ tsp onion powder
* ¼ tsp black pepper

### Tofu ricotta:
* 1 block tofu
* 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
* 1 tbsp lemon juice
* ½ tsp nutritional yeast
* salt
* onion powder
* garlic powder
* white pepper

## Toppings
* olive oil
* spinach
* shiitake mushrooms, sliced
* vegan mozzarella (Miyokos or Numu)
* 1 head of garlic

## Directions
1) Preheat oven to 425°F.
2) In a sauce pan on medium heat, add crushed tomatoes and tomato paste and mix thoroughly.
3) Add salt, sugar, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, oregano, and basil. Mix in and cover for 3 minutes before removing from heat.
4) In a food processor or blender, add tofu, apple cider vinegar, lemon juice, nutritional yeast, salt, onion powder, garlic powder, and white pepper.
5) On a pizza stone or parchment-lined pan, stretch out pizza dough into a rough rectangle. Lightly coat the dough with a thin layer of olive oil.
7) Using a ladle, spread out sauce, then cover with vegan mozarella. Dot the surface with 1-2" wide dabs of ricotta. Finally, spread mushroom slices across the surface.
8) Place the pizza in the oven, along with a head of garlic wrapped in tin foil. 
9) After 12-15 minutes, remove the pizza and garlic. Spread the roasted garlic cloves across the surface and top with rinsed spinach leaves.
