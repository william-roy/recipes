# Tofu Scramble

## Ingredients
* 1 block extra firm tofu
* ½ yellow onion
* ½ red pepper
* 3 cloves garlic
* spinach leaves
* 1 tbsp olive oil
* ¼ tsp turmeric
* black pepper
* ¼ tsp msg
* pinch Kala Namak black salt
* smoked paprika

## Directions
1) Add oil to a large pan and place on medium high heat.
2) Roughly tear apart tofu and add to the pan. Add salt, pepper, turmeric, black salt, msg, and paprika. Cook for 5 minutes.
3) Add onion, garlic, and red pepper. Cook for another 5-10 minutes.
4) Taste, adjust spices and seasonings as needed.
5) Rinse spinach leaves, add to pan and cover for 3 minutes.
6) Uncover and mix. Serve warm.
