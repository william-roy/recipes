# Charred Spicy Broccolini

## Ingredients
* 2 large bunches of broccolini
* olive oil
* salt
* fresh ground black pepper
* 1½ tsp soy sauce
* 1 tbsp honey (or agave syrup)
* 1 tsp chili garlic sauce
* 1 tsp peanut butter

## Directions
1) Preheat oven to 475°F and toss broccolini in olive oil, a pinch of salt, and a pinch of pepper
2) Spread on a baking sheet and roast for 8-10 minutes or until slightly charred and crispy
3) Whisk together soy sauce, honey, chili-garlic sauce, and peanut butter
4) Remove from oven, toss in sauce and serve
