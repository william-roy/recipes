# Baby Bok Choy with Garlic

## Ingredients
* 1 tbsp olive oil
* 3 garlic cloves, chopped
* 1 shallot, chopped
* 1 pound baby bok choy leaves with stems, rinsed
* 1 tbsp soy sauce

## Directions
1) Heat oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add garlic and shallot and cook, stirring, until fragrant.
2) Add bok choy, soy sauce, and 2 tbsp water and cover immediately. Cook 1 minute. 
3) Uncover and toss, then cover and cook until bok choy is tender at the core, about 2 more minutes.
