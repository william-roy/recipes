# Mushroom Fried Rice

## Ingredients
* 2 cups button mushrooms, sliced
* 1 block tempeh, sliced
* ¼ tsp sesame oil
* ½ onion, diced
* 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine
* 1 tbsp canola oil
* 1 cup dry brown rice
* 1 bunch scallions

## Sauce
* 1 tbsp hot water
* 1 tbsp soy sauce
* ½ tsp MSG
* ¼ tsp sugar
* ⅛ tsp white pepper
* 1 tsp salt

## Directions
1) Cook brown rice, remove from heat and let cool.
2) In a small bowl, combine hot water, soy sauce, salt, MSG, sugar, and white pepper.
3) In a small pan, add sesame oil and fry tempeh until golden brown and crispy.
4) Heat a large pan (or wok) over medium high heat. Add oil, onions, and white part of scallions. Cook until onions begin to brown.
5) Add mushrooms and Shaoxing wine, cook until mushrooms are lightly browned.
6) Add rice and mix in thoroughly, allow rice to warm. Add tempeh.
7) Add sauce, continuing to mix until it's evenly distributed.
8) Add green parts of scallions, mix in and cook for another minute or so.
