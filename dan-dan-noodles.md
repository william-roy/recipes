# Dan Dan Noodles

## Ingredients
* fresh thick shanghai noodles
* 1 large carrot, julienned
* 1 bunch scallions, chopped
* ½ lb yu choy
* peanuts, chopped

### Tofu
* 1 block of tofu
* ⅓ cup sui mi ya cai
* ½ tsp five spice powder
* 1 tsp soy sauce
* 2 tsp shaoxing wine
* 2 tsp hoisin sauce

### Sauce
* ½ cup lao gan ma spicy chili crisp
* ½ tsp freshly ground szechuan peppercorn
* ¼ tsp five spice powder
* 3 tbsp soy sauce
* 2 tbsp sesame paste
* 2 tsp sugar
* 2 cloves garlic, finely minced
* ¼ hot water from noodles

## Directions
1) Combine the sauce ingredients (except water) in a bowl and set aside
2) Add oil to a non-stick pan on medium-high heat. Press tofu and crumble it into the pan. Sautee it for about 5 minutes, mixing occasionally.
3) Add shaoxing wine, soy sauce, hoisin sauce, five spice powder, peanuts, and ya cai to the pan. Stir and cook for another 5 minutes. 
4) Add noodles to pot of boiling water and cook for 4-6 minutes, until tender. Take a ¼ cup of water from the pot and mix it into the sauce you made earlier.
5) Transfer noodles out of water into a large bowl and transfer yu choy into the hot water.
6) Add tofu, sauce, carrots, and scallions to the noodles and mix thoroughly.
7) Finally, add yu choy to the bowl and mix. Serve asap.
