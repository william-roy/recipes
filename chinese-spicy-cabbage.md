# Chinese Spicy Cabbage

## Ingredients
* 1 head of cabbage
* 3 tbsp canola oil
* 3 cloves garlic, chopped
* 1 tsp freshly ground Szechuan peppercorn
* 2-3 small red chili peppers, chopped

### Sauce
* 1 tbsp soy sauce
* ½ tbsp Chinese black vinegar
* 1 tsp sugar
* ¼ tsp salt

## Directions
1) Halve the cabbage and tear leaves into 2-3" pieces. Rinse.
2) Whisk together soy sauce, vinegar, sugar, and salt. Set aside.
3) Add oil, chili peppers, and Szechuan peppercorn to pan and turn heat to low. Sautee until fragrant.
4) Add garlic and stir. Sautee until fragrant.
5) Add cabbage and raise heat to medium. Cook until tender.
6) Add sauce and stir. Raise to high heat and cook for 1 minute.
7) Quickly transfer to bowl and serve.
